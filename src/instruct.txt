Smart Hotel Management & Reservation System (SHMRS)
1. Detailed Problem Statement
Business Background

A luxury hotel chain such as Taj, Marriott, Hyatt, or ITC operates multiple room categories and serves different customer types (regular and VIP customers).

The hotel currently manages:

Room inventory manually
Customer registrations
Booking confirmations
Billing and payment transactions
Service requests (Spa, Laundry, Restaurant)
Employee operations
Guest notifications
File-based audit and backup systems

The management wants a centralized Smart Hotel Management & Reservation System (SHMRS) built using Core Java.

Business Objectives

The system must:

Customer Management
Register customers.
Maintain customer information.
Store passport details.
Support VIP customers.
Track additional services used by guests.
Room Management
Maintain Deluxe, Premium, and Suite rooms.
Track room availability.
Calculate room prices dynamically.
Booking Management
Reserve rooms.
Prevent double booking.
Handle cancellations.
Maintain booking status lifecycle.
Payment Management

Support multiple payment mechanisms:

UPI
Credit Card
Debit Card
Cash
Notification Management

Send:

Email notifications
SMS notifications

for:

Booking confirmation
Cancellation
Important announcements
Staff Management

Manage staff:

Managers
Receptionists
File Management

Generate:

Customer folios
Booking reports
Serialized backups
Audit & Recovery

Provide:

Object serialization
Binary backups
Random access ledger updates
Multithreading

Allow multiple customers to book rooms simultaneously.
